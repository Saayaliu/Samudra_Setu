Our Mission

Samudra Setu, which translates to "Ocean Bridge," was founded with a singular vision: to create connections between disparate ocean data sources and transform them into actionable insights for researchers, conservationists, and policymakers.

We believe that by unifying oceanographic, fisheries, and biodiversity data through advanced AI analytics, we can address critical challenges facing our marine ecosystems and promote sustainable ocean management.

Our platform serves as a bridge between cutting-edge technology and marine conservation, empowering stakeholders with the tools needed to make data-driven decisions for our planet's future.

Our Values

Collaboration

Our Impact

Measuring our contribution to ocean science and conservation

45+ Research Institutions Partnered
12M+ Data Points Analyzed
78 Countries Using Our Platform
150+ Publications Supported

Since our founding, Samudra Setu has become a trusted platform for marine researchers worldwide. Our tools have been instrumental in identifying new marine protected areas, predicting harmful algal blooms, and tracking the impacts of climate change on ocean ecosystems.

We're proud to support conservation efforts that have led to the protection of over 500,000 square kilometers of marine habitat and have contributed to sustainable fisheries management practices across multiple regions.
